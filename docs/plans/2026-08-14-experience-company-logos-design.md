# Experience Company Logos Design

## Goal

Make each homepage experience visibly associated with its company while preserving the compact academic layout.

## Approved layout

Use one compact, corresponding experience row per company inside the existing Experience section:

- a fixed-size logo area on the left;
- the existing date, role, company, and work description on the right;
- a subtle divider between the two rows;
- no large cards, shadows, or decorative backgrounds beyond a quiet logo frame.

The outer Experience heading and the existing Contact / News / Experience academic section grid remain unchanged.

## Brand assets

- Tencent Hunyuan uses the official blue circular SVG mark from `hunyuan.tencent.com`.
- ByteDance Seed reuses the existing official `images/papers/seed-wordmark.png` asset.
- Each logo links to its official company page and has accessible alternative text.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: use a consistent logo frame approximately 104 by 46 pixels, with the experience copy in the remaining width.
- Mobile: reduce the logo column while keeping logo and copy on the same row; allow the copy to wrap naturally.
- Preserve adequate separation without increasing the Experience section into card-like blocks.

## Content scope

- Keep both existing experience descriptions unchanged.
- Do not change biography, research interests, news, Selected Research, dates, roles, or project wording.

## Verification

- The Experience section contains exactly two branded rows.
- Tencent Hunyuan and ByteDance Seed each have one corresponding logo and official link.
- Both existing experience descriptions remain present verbatim.
- Desktop and 390px mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow.
- Both logos load successfully in the generated and deployed pages.
