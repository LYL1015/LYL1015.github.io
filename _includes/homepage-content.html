Welcome to my academic homepage. I am Yunlong Lin, a Master's student at Xiamen University (XMU) @SmartDSP, advised by Prof. Xinghao Ding and Yue Huang. I am currently a research intern at ByteDance Seed and previously interned at Tencent Hunyuan.

Research Interests

I work on long-horizon agents and the systems that help them learn and improve, especially:

(i) Long-horizon agents: CLI and open-ended workflows with JarvisHub, Claw-Eval-Live, and JarvisX-Cowork.

(ii) Agentic RL & harness generalization: trajectory rollout, rubric and verifier design, feedback-driven learning, and harness generalization in Seed2.0, JarvisIR, JarvisArt, and Gen-Searcher.

(iii) Self-improving agents / RSI: Auto R&D and AI-for-AI through skill, data, rubric, and harness evolution in the Seed2.1 Auto R&D Agent ("Seed-for-Seed") and JarvisEvo.

Welcome to contact me for discussions and collaborations on agents, reinforcement learning, and recursive self-improvement.

Open-Source Project
JarvisX-Cowork: A Personal AI Creative Assistant for End-to-End Creative Workflows An open-source creative assistant that supports end-to-end workflows from web reference search to image generation, refinement, video creation, and polished demos, decks, or docs.
