# Long-Horizon Agents and RSI Homepage Design

## Goal

Reframe the complete homepage around long-horizon agents, agentic reinforcement learning, harness design, and recursive self-improvement (RSI), while treating visual and multimodal domains only as environments, interfaces, state, or feedback sources.

## Narrative hierarchy

The homepage follows one continuous research story:

1. long-horizon agents operate in open-ended environments;
2. agent systems and harnesses provide persistent state, memory, tools, and reliable execution;
3. agentic RL uses trajectories, environment feedback, rubrics, and verifiers to improve behavior;
4. self-improving agents and RSI evolve skills, data, evaluators, and harnesses through Auto R&D and AI-for-AI.

The introductory paragraph defines this identity immediately. The Research Interests section expands it into three concise directions:

- **Long-horizon agent systems & harnesses**;
- **Agentic RL & feedback**;
- **Self-improving agents / RSI**.

Avoid positioning VLMs, multimodal learning, image generation, restoration, or retouching as research themes. When a project operates in a visual or creative domain, describe that domain as the agent's environment, tool interface, persistent state, or feedback channel.

## Content boundaries

- Preserve official paper and project titles, even when an official title contains terms such as photo editing, image restoration, or image generation.
- Preserve all existing project, paper, code, media, and report links.
- Preserve factual venue badges and project images.
- Rewrite the biography, Research Interests copy, News copy, and every project insight to support the approved narrative.
- Remove the phrase “vision-language agent” and avoid new uses of “VLM,” “multimodal,” or equivalent research positioning.

## Selected Research order

Order the projects by their relevance to the homepage narrative:

1. ByteDance Seed: Agent Improvement & Auto R&D
2. JarvisHub
3. JarvisEvo

5. JarvisX-Cowork

The ByteDance Seed card leads with Seed2.1 Auto R&D / “Seed-for-Seed” and Seed2.0 harness generalization. The remaining cards move from agent harnesses and self-evolution through live evaluation and long-horizon workflows to domain-specific agent environments.

## News

Keep News concise and evidence-oriented:

- JarvisHub as an open harness for long-horizon agents;
- Claw-Eval-Live as a live benchmark for evolving workflows;
- JarvisEvo as self-evolving agent research and its CVPR 2026 acceptance;
- JarvisArt as a long-horizon tool-using agent and its NeurIPS 2025 acceptance.

Remove the duplicate JarvisEvo release item and avoid application-first photo-retouching language.

## Experience

Use reverse chronological order:

1. ByteDance Seed, Jan 2026 – Present;
2. Tencent Hunyuan, Jun 2025 – Dec 2025.

Each row contains:

- a directly displayed company logo in a fixed alignment column;
- a primary line for date, role, and team;
- a concise secondary line for the agent research focus.

Remove the logo container's background, border, radius, and padding. Use a transparent official Seed wordmark so neither company mark appears on a rectangular backing. Keep both logos linked to the official company pages and maintain the same-row responsive layout.

## Visual scope

Retain the current academic typography, rules, spacing, colors, Selected Research row layout, navigation, and profile presentation. This is a narrative and hierarchy revision, not a new visual theme.
